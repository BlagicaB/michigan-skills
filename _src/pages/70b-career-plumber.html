How to become a plumber in Michigan

To become a plumber in Michigan, you register as a plumbing apprentice, work 6,000 hours over at least 3 years under a licensed plumber, then pass the state's written and hands-on journey plumber exams at 18 or older. Union apprenticeships through the UA are free and paid. Michigan plumbers earn a median of $38.56 an hour.

Last checked: October 2026. Pay is the Michigan median from the federal government (May 2025).

Free and paid, usually 5 years. Detroit example: 10,000 job hours plus school one day every other week. Detroit, Local 98 · West Michigan, Local 174 · Lansing, Local 333 · Flint, Local 370 · Kalamazoo, Local 357 · Saginaw, Local 85

Non-union employers

Plumbing contractors also register apprentices. Michigan Works! can help connect you and may help pay for training.
