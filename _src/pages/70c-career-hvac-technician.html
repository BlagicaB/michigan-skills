How to become an HVAC technician in Michigan

Michigan has no individual HVAC technician license. To start, you need EPA Section 608 certification to handle refrigerant, plus training from a community college program or an apprenticeship, and you work under a licensed mechanical contractor. HVAC techs earn a median of $29.26 an hour, with about 1,360 openings a year.

Last checked: October 2026. Pay is the Michigan median from the federal government (May 2025).

Ways to get trained, cheap or free

In high school

Some career tech centers teach HVAC or building trades. CTE guide

Community college

Many Michigan community colleges offer HVAC certificates that prepare you for EPA 608. Free for many adults through Reconnect. Find a college

Union apprenticeship

UA pipefitters train HVAC service techs, free and paid. Detroit, Local 636 (5 years, tuition free) · Lansing, Local 333 (5 years, starts at 45% of journeyman pay) · Flint, Local 370. Ann Arbor's Local 190 isn't taking HVAC apprentices right now.

Short training for this job may qualify for Workforce Pell once Michigan approves specific programs, so ask the provider.
