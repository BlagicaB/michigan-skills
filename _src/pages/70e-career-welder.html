How to become a welder in Michigan

Michigan has no state welding license. Employers hire on skill and code certifications, usually from the American Welding Society (AWS), which you earn by passing a hands-on test. You can train at a career tech center in high school, a community college certificate or a union apprenticeship. Welders earn a median of $24.03 an hour.

Last checked: October 2026. Pay is the Michigan median from the federal government (May 2025).

Ways to get trained, cheap or free

In high school

Welding is one of the most common career tech center programs, and many students leave with AWS certifications. CTE guide

Community college

Example: Macomb Community College's Basic Welding Certificate is 23 credits (384 hours), with an advanced certificate after. Free for many adults through Reconnect. Find a college

Union apprenticeship

Welding is a core skill in several trades: Boilermakers Local 169 (4 years, 6,000 hours) · Ironworkers Local 25 · Pipefitters Local 636. Union welders who do pipe or structural work usually earn more than the median.

Short training for this job may qualify for Workforce Pell once Michigan approves specific programs, so ask the provider.
