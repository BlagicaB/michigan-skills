How to get a CDL and become a truck driver in Michigan

To get a Class A CDL in Michigan, you get a commercial learner's permit from the Secretary of State, finish federally required entry-level driver training with a registered provider, hold the permit at least 14 days, then pass the skills test. You can drive inside Michigan at 18 but must be 21 to cross state lines. Training often takes about 3 weeks. Truck drivers earn a median of $27.49 an hour, with about 6,650 openings a year.

Last checked: October 2026. Pay is the Michigan median from the federal government (May 2025).

Ways to get trained, cheap or free

The steps

1. Get a DOT medical card. 2. Pass the knowledge tests for a commercial learner's permit (80% to pass). 3. Finish entry-level driver training with a provider on the federal Training Provider Registry. 4. Hold the permit 14 days. 5. Pass the skills test with an approved tester.

Cost

Community college examples run about $5,200 for Class A (Mid Michigan College and Lake Michigan College, about 3 weeks). These short courses usually can't use FAFSA aid.

Who can help pay

Michigan Works! may cover much or all of it if you qualify. Some trucking companies train new hires in exchange for a work commitment, so read the contract.

Short training for this job may qualify for Workforce Pell once Michigan approves specific programs, so ask the provider.
