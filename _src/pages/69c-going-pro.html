The Going PRO Talent Fund

The Going PRO Talent Fund is a Michigan grant that helps employers pay for training that leads to an industry-recognized credential, for new hires or current employees. Employers apply through their local Michigan Works! agency. The next round opens in early 2027.

What does Going PRO pay for?

Short-term training that leads to a credential other employers would also recognize: classroom or customized training, registered apprenticeship and on-the-job training. In its July 2026 round, more than $6 million went to 121 businesses to train 2,247 current employees and 550 new hires, including 933 apprentices.

A September 2026 state audit examined how awards are selected and monitored, so expect careful documentation requirements.

Sources: LEO, Going PRO Talent Fund · LEO, July 14, 2026 · Office of the Auditor General

How do employers apply?

  1. Contact your local Michigan Works! business services team now, before the round opens.
  2. Identify the jobs, the credential and the training provider.
  3. Apply with Michigan Works! when the early 2027 round opens.
