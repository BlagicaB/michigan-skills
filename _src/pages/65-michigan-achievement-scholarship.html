The Michigan Achievement Scholarship

The Michigan Achievement Scholarship gives students who finished high school in 2023 or later one of three awards: up to $5,500 a year at a Michigan university, free in-district community college through the Community College Guarantee, or up to $2,000 a year for career training. You file the FAFSA (or a separate application for career training) and enroll within 15 months of graduating.

The three awards

Free tuition

Community college

In-district tuition and mandatory fees for up to three years, no income limit, plus $1,000 for Pell-eligible students. Details

Up to $2,000 a year

Career training

Up to two years toward training programs of 150+ hours, like CDL, welding, HVAC or medical certificates. Since 2026-27 it can be combined with the other award in the same year.

Who qualifies for the Michigan Achievement Scholarship?

  • A high school diploma, certificate of completion or equivalency earned in 2023 or later.
  • Michigan residency since July 1 of the previous year. You no longer have to have graduated from a Michigan high school (as of 2026-27).
  • U.S. citizen, permanent resident or approved refugee.
  • Enrolled within 15 months of graduating, full time for the college awards.
  • Not in default on a federal student loan.

How do you apply?

For college: file the FAFSA. That's the application. For career training: apply through the MiSSG Student Portal from MI Student Aid. Questions go to MI Student Aid at 888-447-2687.
