Michigan Reconnect

Michigan Reconnect pays in-district community college tuition for Michigan adults 21 and older who don't have a college degree. It covers associate degrees and skill certificates, including most trades certificates, and pays what's left after Pell and other aid. The age dropped from 25 to 21 in the 2026-27 state budget.

Last checked: October 2026 against Michigan Reconnect (MiLEAP).

How longUntil you finish an associate degree or four years pass
To keep itAt least 12 credits per academic year and a 2.0 GPA

Apply soon if you're 21 to 24. The lower age was set through the state budget, not a permanent change to the law, so it isn't guaranteed past this budget year.

Who qualifies for Michigan Reconnect?

  • You're 21 or older when you apply.
  • You've lived in Michigan for at least a year.
  • You have a high school diploma, GED or certificate of completion. No diploma yet? Start with free adult education.
  • You don't have an associate or bachelor's degree.
  • You file the FAFSA.

What does Reconnect pay for?

In-district tuition, mandatory fees and contact-hour charges, after Pell and other state aid. It doesn't cover books, supplies, application or graduation fees, or the extra cost of an out-of-district college, where you get a large discount instead. It works for trades certificates like welding, HVAC, electrical technology and CNC machining, as well as associate degrees.

Results so far: more than 250,000 people have applied, nearly 20,000 have graduated and graduates' median pay rose $13,400 a year after finishing.

How do you apply?

  1. Fill out the Reconnect application. It takes about 5 minutes.
  2. Apply to a community college. Find yours.
  3. File the FAFSA.
  4. Enroll. Free Reconnect navigators can help along the way.

Common questions

What is the age limit for Michigan Reconnect?

You must be 21 or older for 2026-27. The age was lowered from 25 to 21 in the state budget, so confirm before you apply in future years.

Source: Michigan Reconnect

Does Michigan Reconnect cover trade programs?

Yes. Reconnect covers Pell-eligible skill certificates as well as associate degrees, which includes many trades programs like welding, HVAC and electrical technology at Michigan community colleges.

Source: Michigan Reconnect
